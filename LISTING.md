# Marketplace listing — Pulse Analytics

The text below is what the Framer Marketplace form gets. Keep it in step with
the plugin; the form has no version control, this file does.

**Name:** Pulse Analytics

**Byline (one line):** Privacy-first analytics for your Framer site. One click, no cookies, no banner.

**Description:**

Pulse is privacy-first web analytics from Ciphera, a Belgian company. It
measures visitors without cookies or personal data, so your site needs no
consent banner for it, and the script is under 3 KB.

This plugin adds Pulse to every published page of your site without touching
Site Settings:

- Enter your site's domain (it is prefilled from your published URL) and click
  Install Pulse.
- Optionally track clicks, copies and form submits as well.
- The panel shows what is installed and where, and tells you if the snippet was
  switched off in Site Settings.
- Change the domain or remove Pulse from the window menu. Your other custom
  code is left exactly as it is.

You need a Pulse account with a site registered for the same domain. Sign up
and add your site at pulse.ciphera.net; the free plan is enough to start.
